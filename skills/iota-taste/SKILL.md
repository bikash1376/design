# iOTA taste

Generated UI converges on one average page. These steps pull a page away from it.

## Steps
1. **Name the page's one job** in a sentence. The hierarchy follows from it.
2. **Choose a layout on purpose.** Write down two layouts that are not the default stack of hero, three cards and a CTA, then pick one and say why.
3. **Use real content.** Real copy, real numbers, real product shots. A placeholder hides whether the design works.
4. **Spend one bold move.** One thing may be loud: the type, an image, or the motion. Everything else stays quiet.
5. **Inspect it.** Render at 1440, 768 and 390. Fix the three worst things, then look again, up to three rounds.

## Never
- Purple gradients, glass cards, emoji as icons, or a badge above every heading.
- Three equal icon cards for three numbers.
- Text touching an edge, or margins that differ left and right.
- Filler words: seamless, robust, effortless, leverage. Name the mechanism or the number.
- Grey text on a colored surface.

## Finish
A page feels designed through small details: optical alignment, tabular numerals, a press state on every control, a real focus ring and a considered empty state. Do these last, and do all of them.
